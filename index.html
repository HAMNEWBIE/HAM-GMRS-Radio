GMRS

462 / 467 MHz

The General Mobile Radio Service shares its 22 channels with FRS. A GMRS license costs $35, lasts 10 years, requires no exam, and covers your whole immediate family. FRS use of the same channels needs no license at lower power.

Mesh networks

902–928 MHz ISM / 2.4 + 5.8 GHz

Two different worlds that both call themselves mesh. One is license-free text messaging over LoRa, the other is licensed high-bandwidth IP networking on repurposed WiFi gear.

LORA Meshtastic

Off-grid, encrypted text and position sharing between cheap LoRa nodes (Heltec, RAK, LILYGO, around $30 each). Runs in the 902–928 MHz ISM band in the US, so no license is required. Range is line-of-sight: a node on a hill routes for everyone below it.

meshtastic.org

AREDN Amateur mesh

Ham-licensed data networking on modified WiFi hardware in the 2.4 and 5.8 GHz amateur allocations. Real IP bandwidth for cameras, VoIP, and services between nodes. Requires at least a Technician license, and Part 97 traffic cannot be encrypted.

arednmesh.org
