Embed this on your site

Put a live, playable card on your club page, blog, or personal site. No account, no key, nothing to install.

  1. Pick the net type above. The code below follows your choice.
  2. Press Copy embed code.
  3. Paste it into your page's HTML. On WordPress use a Custom HTML block; on most other site builders look for an HTML or Embed block. If you edit raw HTML files, paste it wherever the card should appear.
  4. Publish. Visitors get a playable card right on your page.
<iframe src="https://hamnewbie.github.io/HAM-GMRS-Radio/bingo.html?embed=1&deck=ham"
  width="100%" height="980" style="border:0" title="Net Bingo"></iframe>

Tweaks: every visitor gets a fresh shuffle by default. To hand everyone the identical card (one card per net night), add &seed=123456 inside the src, using any number you like: same number, same card. Change deck=ham to deck=gmrs, deck=wx, or deck=skills by hand if you prefer. If the card gets cut off on your page, raise the height number.

Lingo decoder

What you just heard, for anyone new to the net.

73
ham-speak for best regards, goodbye
QSL
received and understood (CW shorthand)
Double
two stations at once, both heard, neither understood
Kerchunk
keying up without IDing (don't)
Picket-fencing
signal flutter while moving
Elmer
a mentor ham
Net control
runs the net, follow their lead
Traffic
messages to pass; no traffic means just saying hi
SWR
antenna health; high is bad
Timeout timer
repeater cutoff for the long-winded
