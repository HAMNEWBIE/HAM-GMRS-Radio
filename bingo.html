Embed this on your site

Put a live, playable card on your club page, blog, or personal site, and point your players there instead of here. Your net, your site, your game night. No account, no key, nothing to install. What it does for a net or club:

  • Traffic to your site, every net night. The game lives on your page, so your address is the one read on the air.
  • Giveaways that hold up. Card numbers make winners verifiable, so prizes and door-prize drawings are easy to run straight.
  • A friendlier front door for the hobby. Lurkers get something to do while they listen, and newcomers learn the culture one square at a time.
  1. Pick the net type above. The code below follows your choice.
  2. Press Copy embed code.
  3. Paste it into your page's HTML. On WordPress use a Custom HTML block; on most other site builders look for an HTML or Embed block. If you edit raw HTML files, paste it wherever the card should appear.
  4. Publish. Visitors get a playable card right on your page.
<iframe src="https://wsnj234.github.io/amrad/bingo.html?embed=1&deck=ham"
  width="100%" height="980" style="border:0" title="Net Bingo"></iframe>

Tweaks: every visitor gets a fresh shuffle by default. To hand everyone the identical card (one card per net night), add &seed=123456 inside the src, using any number you like: same number, same card. Change deck=ham to deck=gmrs, deck=wx, or deck=skills by hand if you prefer. If the card gets cut off on your page, raise the height number.

Here is how to introduce it

Once the card is on your site, or you're just sharing the link, this is a script net control can read on the air, about a minute of airtime. Square brackets are yours to fill in or drop.

“Before we get started tonight, we're trying something fun on [NET NAME]: Net Bingo. Grab your phone and go to [YOUR WEBSITE / tinyurl.com/wsnj234]. You'll get a random card full of things that, let's be honest, happen on every net. Somebody doubles. Somebody's roger beep is still on. Somebody reports weather that already happened. When you hear one actually happen tonight, tap that square. Five in a row, any direction, is bingo.”

“Two rules. The center square is free, you earned it by showing up. And no calling bingo during check-ins. When we get to [THE COMMENTS ROUND / THE END OF THE NET], come back with your callsign and claim it. Every card has a card number at the top. Give us that number with your claim, because the site can regenerate your exact card from it, so wins are checkable. [Tonight's winner gets PRIZE.]”

“If you'd rather play on paper, hit Print on the site before we start and mark squares with a pen. That's it. Good luck, and try not to be the reason somebody marks a square.”

Net control crib notes: keep the spoken address short. Best is your own site with the card embedded. No site? The short link tinyurl.com/wsnj234 goes straight to the cards and survives being read on the air. Either way, put the link in the club email or group chat before the net so nobody has to copy a URL by ear. When someone claims bingo, ask for their card number, punch it in below, and check the claimed line against what actually happened on the net. Winners can also press Save your winning card and send you the image, which is stamped with the card number, marked count, and save time.
